# EchoSphere Sales AI - Pricing Guide

## Overview
EchoSphere offers flexible, transparent, and scalable subscription tiers tailored for sales organizations, SDR teams, and enterprise contact centers. All subscriptions include sub-500ms voice response latency, bi-directional interruption handling, and continuous conversation memory.

---

## Subscription Tiers

### 1. Starter Tier
- **Price**: $29 per user / month (billed monthly) or $24 per user / month (billed annually, ~20% discount).
- **Target Audience**: Startups, boutique sales teams, and solo entrepreneurs (1 to 9 users).
- **Included Features**:
  - Up to 1,000 conversational voice minutes per month per user.
  - Basic CRM integration (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Google Contacts).
  - Out-of-the-box knowledge base retrieval (up to 50 documents).
  - Automated meeting scheduling via Google Calendar.
  - Standard email support with 24-hour response SLA.
- **Overage**: $0.05 per additional voice minute.

### 2. Growth Tier (Most Popular)
- **Price**: $59 per user / month (billed monthly) or $49 per user / month (billed annually).
- **Target Audience**: Scaling mid-market sales teams and B2B growth organizations (10 to 49 users).
- **Included Features**:
  - Up to 3,500 conversational voice minutes per month per user.
  - Full CRM synchronization (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, MongoDB native CRM).
  - Real-time BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) qualification scoring.
  - Natural interruption handling with low-latency turn-taking (<450ms).
  - Multi-tier Slack escalation webhooks with customer sentiment tagging.
  - Custom knowledge base chunking and priority vector search.
  - Priority support with 4-hour response SLA.
- **Overage**: $0.035 per additional voice minute.

### 3. Enterprise Tier
- **Price**: $99 per user / month (billed monthly) or $79 per user / month (billed annually for 50+ users).
- **Target Audience**: Large enterprises, outbound call centers, and multinational sales operations (50+ to 1,000+ users).
- **Included Features**:
  - Unlimited voice minutes (fair usage policy: 10,000 mins/seat/month).
  - Custom LLM fine-tuning & proprietary vector embedding pipeline.
  - Custom API integrations, custom webhooks, on-prem or dedicated MongoDB Atlas cluster option.
  - Bi-directional call recording, compliance auditing (SOC2, HIPAA, GDPR compliant).
  - Real-time supervisor barge-in and warm human transfer.
  - Dedicated Technical Account Manager (TAM) and 99.99% uptime SLA.
  - Custom SLA with 15-minute emergency response time.

## Volume Discounts & Enterprise Rules
We reward scale with tiered volume discounts applied to list prices:

- **10 to 49 seats**: 10% volume discount on all licenses.
- **50 to 199 seats**: 15% volume discount on all licenses.
- **200+ seats**: 25% volume discount on all licenses, plus waived setup and onboarding fees.
- **500+ seats**: Custom Enterprise Agreement with custom per-seat pricing (as low as $49/user/month for Enterprise tier) and tailored feature development.

### Example Pricing for 200 Users
- **List Price for Enterprise**: 200 users × $99/mo = $19,800/month.
- **With 200+ Volume Discount (25% off)**: 200 × $74.25 = **$14,850/month**.
- **With Annual Billing Commitment (additional 20% discount)**: **$11,880/month** (effective $59.40 per user/month), billed annually at $142,560/year.
- Includes complimentary enterprise onboarding ($5,000 value waived) and dedicated customer success architect.
